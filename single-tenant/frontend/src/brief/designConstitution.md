MOVA DESIGN CONSTITUTION

1. IBM Carbon is the primary design authority.

2. Do not invent visual patterns when Carbon provides an
   established pattern.

3. Do not introduce a custom component when an equivalent
   Carbon component exists.

4. Do not introduce decorative visual elements without
   an explicit functional purpose.

5. Do not use gradients for product UI unless explicitly
   defined by MOVA brand guidelines.

6. Do not use glassmorphism.

7. Do not use glow effects.

8. Do not use decorative sparkles, emojis, or AI-themed
   visual elements.

9. Do not arbitrarily increase border radius.

10. Do not use color as decoration.

11. Color must communicate hierarchy, state, or action.

12. Icons must communicate meaning.

13. Typography must establish information hierarchy.

14. Spacing must follow the Carbon spacing system.

15. Components must follow Carbon interaction behavior.

16. Domain-specific components may be created only when
    Carbon does not provide the required pattern.

17. Domain components must visually inherit Carbon.

18. Never redesign Carbon components merely to make them
    look more modern.
